I've been to SXSW interactive three 3 years in a row, and I did a good job this year of picking panels that I liked. I put together some of the factors that helped me pick influence my guidelines I've developed based on my experience over the past few years:

However, the facilitator can make or break a panel. A great facilitator can pull quality content out of bland panelists and a terrible facilitator can ruin even the most talented panelists.future15 - these are 15-minute shorties. some are totally worth it. some are not. look at how the session and the participants are described - is it reporting on actual experience or is it more speculation/conjecture? do the participants have experience relevant to the session?beware of self-serving panels. is the sessions going to be focusing entirely on one company and their experience? it may depend on how applicable their experience/organization is to your own. which room is it in? this will give you an idea of how popuplar the SXSW organizers think the session will be. of course there are plenty of packed sessions in small rooms and sparse sessions in giant rooms, but it's a good general guideline. the keynotes, featured sessions and those in the larger ballrooms tend to have a basic level of quality. consider location - is it at or near the main campus?
