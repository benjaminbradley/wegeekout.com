[Job search tips – interview practice with ChatGPT](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1yYBH8c1irc9A5Dx8tEm6wIAOECOoOOYXtGh55XqrPQo/edit?usp=sharing)

This guide covers using ChatGPT (or other LLMs) to run realistic mock interviews: researching the likely interview sequence and interviewer perspectives for a specific job, generating a tailored question set from the JD and your resume, assembling those into a structured prompt, running a live voice-mode mock interview, and then getting structured feedback afterward. It includes practical prompt templates you can copy and adapt directly, as well as general LLM guidance, and related tips/resources.
