Chosen Family Tree

AKA The Flipside Genealogy project

This is an exploratory portal to record and browse how our chosen families (theme camps) have changed over time.

When the site is back up, you’ll be able to register on the site, record your participation (and that of your camp mates / family members) for past events, and browse the history we’ve collected so far, the chosen family tree.
